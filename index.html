US Stocks Momentum & Mean-Reversion Backtest

An algorithmic trading strategy on US stocks (NYSE/NASDAQ common stocks), backtested 2018-2026. This page reports net return, CAGR, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown and profit factor for each start year, an equity curve and the full trade log, plus a daily next-open buy shortlist. Signals are generated by a cross-sectional ML ranker with a market-regime switch (momentum in trends, mean-reversion in chop). Educational research, not investment advice.
GOLDEN (blue) is the standard strategy. RVOL-rescue tabs (purple, 2024+ only) are an experimental reference scenario that relaxes one entry filter slightly; they are shown for comparison, not as the live strategy. $10k start, $11 fee, 26.375% tax. See the About tab for the full methodology.
The RVOL-rescue tabs are NOT the live strategy - golden stays the standard. They show how one extra edge-filter relaxation would have performed in the recent narrow-BULL / volume-spike regime (2024 onward), where it raised net and Sharpe while lowering drawdown. On 2022-2023 golden is better, so this is a regime-dependent reference, not a prediction.
